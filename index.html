How It Works

From a reported problem to a deployed solution, in four steps.

01

Report

Citizens and NGOs submit real problems in their community — from broken infrastructure to public health gaps — with photos, location, and context.

02

Prioritize

The community votes and comments to surface the challenges that matter most, while moderators verify authenticity and urgency.

03

Collaborate

Students, researchers, mentors, and industry partners team up on the highest-priority problems to design workable solutions.

04

Deploy

Vetted solutions move from prototype to pilot, with funding and institutional support to reach the people who reported them.
